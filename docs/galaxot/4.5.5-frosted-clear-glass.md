# 4.5.5 — Frosted Glass and Clear Glass

Branch: `opentune-rebuild-v1`. This increment creates two distinct surface materials within the canonical Kyant Backdrop / Sky renderer. It does not add an extra blur pipeline, activate the appearance selector prematurely, or alter Media3.

## Materials

| Mode | Surface | Edge | Optics |
|---|---|---|---|
| FROSTED | White diffusion 20% dark / 42% light | White 29% dark / 62% light | Blur 10, refraction .035, curve .085, dispersion .001 |
| CLEAR | White transparency 5.5% dark / 10% light | White 48% dark / 38% light | Blur 2, refraction .085, curve .19, dispersion .003 |

The glass renderer owns blur/lens/highlight, fallback and clipping. `GalaxGlassMaterials.kt` owns palettes; `GalaxGlassSurface.kt` forwards them as optional overrides to `GlassEngine.kt`. Default null overrides preserve CLASSIC/BLUR visuals and legacy callers.

Battery saver / low RAM / scrolling surfaces keep tint-and-outline cheap fallback. Android without live backdrop retains SDK-appropriate fallback. Reduced motion constrains expensive optics using 4.5.4 policy. Theme luminance and pure black choose light/dark material tokens; no new saved settings or API changes to legacy consumers.
